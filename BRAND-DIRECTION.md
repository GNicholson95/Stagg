# STAG Systems — brand direction

## Recommendation

Keep **STAG Systems** for the pre-launch phase.

“Systems” is broad enough to cover audio visual, data, telecommunications, CCTV and future areas such as access control, automation or managed network services. The business is more likely to become boxed in by narrow messaging than by the name itself.

Use the name with a clear descriptor:

> **STAG Systems**  
> AV · Data · Telecoms · CCTV

Primary positioning:

> **Connected technology systems for homes and businesses.**

Brand promise:

> **Connected systems. Built to work.**

Before investing in signage, uniforms or a domain, run the exact name and close variations through:

- [ASIC business-name availability](https://connectonline.asic.gov.au/RegistrySearch/faces/landing/NameAvail.jspx)
- [IP Australia TM Checker](https://www.ipaustralia.gov.au/trade-marks/search-existing-trade-marks/tm-checker)
- An Australian domain-name availability check

A preliminary web scan did not show an obvious Gold Coast AV/data company using the exact name, but there are unrelated STAG businesses in Australia and a UK security company using Stag Systems. That makes the formal checks important.

## Positioning

### What STAG is

A connected-technology installer and systems partner that can handle the physical infrastructure and the equipment at each end.

### Who it is for

- Homeowners who want a clean, reliable installation
- Small and medium businesses
- Hospitality, retail and office operators
- Builders, electricians and property teams needing a specialist subcontractor

### Four service pillars

1. **Audio visual** — displays, TV distribution, digital signage and commercial AV
2. **Data and networks** — structured data cabling, Wi-Fi infrastructure, patching and fault finding
3. **Telecommunications** — satellite, television and signal-distribution systems
4. **CCTV and security** — cameras, recording, remote viewing, additions and upgrades

### What makes the offer different

- One point of contact across several connected disciplines
- Practical equipment choices rather than product dumping
- Clean cable paths and considered placement
- A system that is understandable and serviceable after handover

## Brand system

### Visual direction

- **Base:** near-black and graphite
- **Contrast:** warm off-white
- **Accent:** restrained signal amber
- **Style:** technical, precise and minimal rather than “smart-home luxury” or generic trade branding
- **Imagery:** real finished work, cable management, racks, displays and camera placement; avoid generic stock photos once project photography is available

### Voice

- Direct and confident
- Plain English
- Outcome-led rather than product-led
- Specific without making unsupported licence, accreditation or warranty claims

Avoid phrases such as “cutting-edge solutions”, “one-stop shop” and “industry-leading”. Show the work and explain the result instead.

## Business setup order

1. **Validate the name** with ASIC, IP Australia and domain searches.
2. **Register the chosen business name** and confirm the ABN structure used for invoicing.
3. **Secure a branded domain and email**, ideally a short address such as `hello@...` or `projects@...`.
4. **Confirm the licensing model before advertising regulated work.** ACMA states that customer cabling work must be performed by a registered cabler or by an unregistered cabler under appropriate supervision. Queensland requires a security equipment installer licence for installing, repairing, servicing or maintaining equipment that includes audio or visual recording systems. A sole trader doing all security work may not need a separate security-firm licence, but the individual technical licence still matters.
5. **Set up the operating basics:** quoting terms, scope exclusions, invoice template, public-liability insurance, tool/vehicle cover, record keeping and a job-photo process.
6. **Build proof:** photograph every suitable completed job, request short customer reviews and record the problem, scope and result for future case studies.
7. **Launch lean:** website, Google Business Profile, branded email and one business social profile are enough. Do not create channels that will sit empty.

Official licensing references:

- [ACMA — work as a registered cabler](https://www.acma.gov.au/work-registered-cabler)
- [Queensland — security equipment installer licence](https://www.qld.gov.au/community/fair-trading/regulated-industries-licensing-and-legislation/security-industry-regulation/security-technical-licence/apply-for-a-security-equipment-installer-licence)
- [Queensland — security firm licence](https://www.qld.gov.au/community/fair-trading/regulated-industries-licensing-and-legislation/security-industry-regulation/managing-a-security-firm/apply-for-a-security-firm-licence)

## Before website launch

- Replace the personal email address with the branded address
- Confirm the phone number and service area
- Add active registration or licence numbers only after verification
- Remove any service that is not currently offered or appropriately supervised
- Replace at least the hero image with a strong photo of STAG’s own work
- Add two or three genuine reviews or short project examples
- Add privacy, terms and warranty wording appropriate to the final enquiry process
- Run the manual GitHub Pages publishing workflow only after final approval
